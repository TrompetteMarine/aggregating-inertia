% Figure 4. Consensus in the running economy (Example 1: full resets, theta = 0.7, eta = 5, lambda = nu =
% gamma = 1). Panel A: the stationary joint density of the assessment U and processing A, without the
% agents with A = 0, on a logarithmic colour scale. Panel B: the change in the density of assessments after
